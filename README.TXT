This is the final project for grade 10 computer science. 
BlueJ is recommended for launching the program.

This is a console game. Enter letters or numbers to play.

Controls:
 w: move up 
 a: move left
 s: move down
 d: move right
 i: open inventory 
 `: open main menu (all game progress will be deleted)
 
How to play:
 Use w, a, s, d keys to move around.
 Your objective is to escape from the dungeon.
 But be careful, there are dangerous monsters hiding in the darkness.
 If the monster reaches you, you will have to fight it.
 In the dungeon, you can find various items that will help you on your journey.
 
Example of how the game looks:
..................

..................
....########??....
....HP  []<>  ....
########//####....
??????##??##......
//######??##......
??????????##......

 () - exit portal (this is your objective)
 ## - wall (you can't walk or see through them)
 // - door (you can't see through it, but can walk through it. Monsters can't walk through doors)
 [] - player (this is you)
 <> - monster (you should evade them)
 .. - map that you have not yet discovered
 ?? - map that you have discovered but can't see (you can't see items and monsters)
 ii - Candle (increases your view distance)
 HP - Health Potion (restores 20 health)
 RS - Rusty Sword (adds 3-7 damage to your attack)
 SS - Steel Sword (adds 7-12 damage to your attack)
 XG - X-Ray Glasses (allows you to see through walls)
 LA - Leather Armor (gives you 2 armor)
